# Editor `cold`: first day, starts the project from nothing

Board http://127.0.0.1:9300/ (started on a one-clip practice bin) · browser 1536x864 (a laptop) · output `editors/cold/out/`

A person who has cut holiday videos in iMovie and CapCut and has opened Premiere a few times. They
have never seen Roughcut. A friend set it up and said only this:

"Roughcut is open in your browser on a practice project. Your GoPro footage from the ski trip to
Copper Mountain (February 2026) is in the folder /mnt/roughcut/footage/copper-02-2026 on the same
machine. It is an AI editor: it can go through the footage and propose a cut, and you can also cut
by hand or tell it what you want in words. Make a film of the trip, somewhere between 60 and 120
seconds, to send to the friends who were on it."

1. First look, touching nothing: what does the screen tell a newcomer? A welcome, a tour, a start?
2. Start a NEW project from scratch with the footage folder, from the screen alone. Every guess and
   dead end is material.
3. Get the tool to understand the footage and propose a first cut. Read what it says it will do and
   how long before starting; watch and time the wait; explore while waiting.
4. Understand the result: why those shots? Check at least six of its claims against the picture.
   Is it obvious how to accept, reject or change the proposal?
5. Watch the cut, decide the three things you most dislike, fix them the way a newcomer would try
   first (by hand, or in words).
6. Export, find the file, skim it with `film_sheet()`: is it what the Program monitor showed?
7. Throughout: Help, tooltips, the "?" button, any tour. Did they answer the question you had?

Budget: up to about 3 hours of wall clock.
